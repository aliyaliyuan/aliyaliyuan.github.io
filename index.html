01 / 04 · scPrediXcan

Liver cells to
plasma biomarkers

I generated ML gene expression models for 17 liver cell types from single-cell sequencing data that integrated epigenetic features from Enformer. I use these models to explore how genes in each liver cell type regulate plasma proteins, so we can find general biomarkers in blood.

PLASMAXCAN Liver cell-type expression models

Single-cell liver data → pseudobulk → Enformer features → one neural network per cell type → plasma protein regulation.

02 / 04 · JCAST-HF

From transcripts
to proteins

I used an RNA-seq informed proteomics pipeline to identify novel heart failure biomarkers in LVAD patients using personalized alternatively-spliced protein sequence databases.

RNA-SEQ-INFORMED PROTEOMICS · JCAST-HF Personalized protein databases

STAR → rMATS → JCAST turns each patient's alternative-splicing events into protein sequences for mass-spec searches.

03 / 04 · AGING HEART

The aging
heart

I co-authored a study showing that BubR1 insufficiency drives cardiac hypertrophy, fibrosis and senescence. The transcriptomic changes it causes mirror those in aged hearts and in end-stage heart failure.

AGING CELL · 2025 BubR1 & cardiac aging

Transcriptomic profiling links BubR1 loss to ion-channel, cytoskeletal and contractile pathway disruption.

04 / 04 · ASLINGO

ASLingo is a prototype ASL learning tool that helps learners build confidence. It puts the visual side of the language first, with live sign recognition from your camera, and introduces a mini-game: guide a hedgehog around a grocery store and sign each item on your shopping list.

COMPUTER VISION · MEDIAPIPE Try the prototype

Live sign recognition, flashcards and the grocery-store game, right in your browser.
